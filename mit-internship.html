MIT Sea Grant Internship

I interned with MIT Sea Grant, which works on ocean engineering and underwater robotics. Day to day, I helped keep the robotics labs clean and running. On many days, and sometimes for whole weeks, I was given engineering tasks: Arduino coding, soldering, and a lot of CAD in Fusion 360.

Submarine Remote Controller

A class at MIT was learning to build pressure-sensing submarines, and they needed a remote controller to drive them.

I spent a full week on the controller in Fusion 360. I designed it, tested it, and redesigned it based on what worked and what didn't, going through several versions.

Impact Logger for a Robotic Fish

I helped an MIT undergraduate build an impact logger for a 10 lb, 3D-printed fish. The logger records the g-forces the fish experiences.

The electronics ride inside the fish: an Arduino, a motor shield and an acceleration sensor.

I suggested which sensor to use and wrote the Arduino code that reads and logs the g-force data.

Watertight Enclosure & RC Torpedo

I was tasked with designing a 3D-printed container that stays watertight underwater, sealed with O-rings.

In my free time, I took the idea further and tried building a remote-controlled torpedo around the enclosure.
