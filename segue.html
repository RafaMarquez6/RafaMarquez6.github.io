Segue

Segue is a daily music game I designed, like Wordle but for playlists. Every day you get a start song and an end song that sound nothing alike. Your job is to build a playlist that blends smoothly from one to the other.

I built it with help from Claude, an AI assistant. It started as a prototype and grew into a full website with accounts and a daily leaderboard.

How it works

How to play

  1. Get today's pair. Everyone gets the same start and end song each day, like Iron Man by Black Sabbath to Flowers by Miley Cyrus.
  2. Beat the clock. You have 3 minutes to add 3 to 8 songs in between.
  3. Score smooth steps. Each song is a dot on the sound map. Small steps toward the end song score high, and big jumps or steps backward lose points.
  4. Compare. Your run goes on the daily leaderboard, with a bonus for picking songs nobody else chose.

What's under the hood

The sound map

Every song sits on a two-axis map: guitars to synths, and gentle to heavy. Genres like Metal, Pop, EDM and Folk each have their own region.

A growing song catalog

Right now, 181 songs are placed on the map by hand. Next, Python scripts will collect songs from Billboard charts and music databases and score each one's position ahead of time, so the game runs fast with no AI while people play.

Accounts and leaderboard

Players sign in, pick a DJ name, earn holiday badges and see the daily board, all through a Supabase database.

Extra modes

Hard Cut is a risky move for bonus points, and Infinite mode lets you keep playing new pairs. Missing songs can be requested and get added every week.
